# How to pay

HashChat accepts **crypto only**: Monero (preferred), Bitcoin and Lightning.
No account, no email, no name. Every invoice has a fresh address, so nobody
can see the project's balance or link your payment to anyone else's.

> **Honest note:** buying and holding crypto is legal in most places, but
> rules differ. Follow the law where you live, including any tax rules.
> The maintainer declares this income where required.

## 1. Get Monero (XMR) peer-to-peer: Haveno (recommended)

1. Install a Haveno network client (for example from <https://haveno.exchange>,
   check the signatures) and let it start its own Tor connection.
2. Create a local wallet inside the app and write down the seed on paper.
3. Pick a "Buy XMR" offer that uses a payment method you already have
   (bank transfer, cash by mail, etc.).
4. Take the offer, pay the seller as shown, then confirm. The XMR is
   released from escrow to your Haveno wallet.
5. Withdraw to your own wallet (Feather or Cake Wallet).

## 2. Or get Bitcoin peer-to-peer

- **Bisq** (<https://bisq.network>): desktop app, peer-to-peer over Tor,
  multisig escrow. Good for larger amounts.
- **RoboSats** (<https://learn.robosats.com>): over Tor, pays out on
  Lightning. Fast and cheap for small amounts like a monthly plan.

## 3. Optional: swap BTC to XMR

- **Feather Wallet** (desktop, <https://featherwallet.org>) or **Cake
  Wallet** (mobile, <https://cakewallet.com>) can swap BTC to XMR in the app.
- You can also pay the invoice in BTC or Lightning directly; Monero is
  preferred because it keeps both sides private.

## 4. Pay the invoice

1. Open the support page (see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md)) and choose a plan,
   or any amount for pay-what-you-want.
2. The self-hosted payment page shows a fresh address and the exact amount.
3. Send from your own wallet. Wait for confirmations (Monero: about
   10 blocks, ~20 minutes; Lightning: instant).
4. Download your license file. It holds only the plan, the expiry and a
   random order number: nothing about you. Keep it; the app checks it
   offline and never contacts a server.

Prepaid packs (3, 6 or 12 months) avoid monthly payments. The app shows a
local reminder 14 days before the license expires.

Pay-what-you-want donations need no license and change nothing in the app.
